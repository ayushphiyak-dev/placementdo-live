Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PlacementDo ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our AI-powered mock interview platform at www.placementdo.app.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you sign up.
  • Interview Data: Audio recordings, text transcripts, and CV/resume documents you upload during mock interview sessions. This data is used exclusively to generate real-time AI feedback and performance reports.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, session duration, and device/browser information collected automatically for analytics and performance monitoring.
  • Cookies: We use first-party and third-party cookies as described below.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses the DART cookie to serve ads to our users based on their prior visits to our site and other sites on the Internet. This allows Google and its partners to serve ads based on a user's interests and browsing activity.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the PlacementDo platform and AI interview coaching service.
  • To generate your personalized post-interview performance report and scoring breakdown.
  • To send service-related communications, billing receipts, and product updates.
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense on our free tier.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

5. Data Retention

Interview reports and performance metrics are retained as long as your account is active, allowing you to track your progress over time. You may request account deletion at any time by emailing support@placementdo.app. This will permanently remove all associated CVs, transcripts, and personal data from our active databases within 30 days.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for all data in transit. We do not use your personal interview data to train public AI models. All audio is processed ephemerally and transcribed securely via enterprise-grade LLM partners.

7. Children's Privacy

PlacementDo is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@placementdo.app.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@placementdo.app
