Contact PlacementDo

For product support, accessibility feedback, partnerships, content corrections, or questions about this website, email support@placementdo.app.

What to include

For a support request, include the page or feature you were using, the device and browser, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or confidential interview material by email.

For a privacy request, identify the account email or the data involved and write “Privacy request” in the subject. We may ask for reasonable verification before making changes to an account or disclosing personal information. See the Privacy Policy for collection, retention, cookies, and deletion details.

Before you write

Company hiring patterns, eligibility rules, and assessment dates change. If your question concerns a particular employer, include the official job description or assessment notice so the information can be checked against the current source. PlacementDo provides educational practice and cannot guarantee an interview or job outcome.
