About PlacementDo

PlacementDo is an interview-practice and placement-preparation service for students, recent graduates, and other early-career candidates. It brings together written preparation guides and interactive mock interviews so visitors can study a concept, practise explaining it, and review what to improve next.

The product is built around a simple idea: preparation should be observable. A candidate can read about a coding pattern or the STAR method, try an answer aloud, and then compare the result with a clear checklist. The platform is intended to support that loop, not to replace coursework, a mentor, an employer's instructions, or a candidate's own judgement.

What you can do here

Public guides cover aptitude, coding, behavioural interviews, resumes, company research, and planning a placement season. The interactive experience can be configured for a target role and interview style. Depending on the route and account state, it may ask follow-up questions, record the practice context you provide, and present feedback for your review. The exact availability of features can change as the service develops.

Useful practice starts with evidence from your own work. We encourage visitors to use their real projects, explain trade-offs honestly, and verify time-sensitive company details against official job descriptions and assessment instructions. AI-generated questions or feedback are suggestions for practice; they are not a hiring decision and do not predict an offer.

Our approach to trust

We aim to publish original, practical material rather than scraped question lists. When a topic depends on a company's current process, the guide says so and points candidates back to official sources. We avoid promises of selection, invented testimonials, and claims about results that we cannot substantiate.

Privacy is part of the preparation experience. Before using account or interview features, read the Privacy Policy to understand what information may be processed, how optional cookies are controlled, and how to request deletion. The Terms of Service explain acceptable use and the limits of AI-generated guidance.

Questions or corrections

If you find an outdated placement pattern, a broken link, or a privacy question, contact support@placementdo.app. Include the page URL and enough context for us to investigate. We welcome specific corrections and will review them against reliable, current sources.
