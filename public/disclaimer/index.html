PlacementDo provides educational interview and placement-preparation resources. The guides, examples, mock questions, and AI-generated feedback are for practice and general information; they are not legal, financial, medical, recruitment, or career advice.

No hiring or performance guarantee

Using PlacementDo does not guarantee an interview, selection, salary, promotion, or other career result. Hiring decisions depend on the employer, role, assessment, eligibility, competition, and your own preparation. A score or suggestion from an AI session is not an employer assessment and may be incomplete or incorrect.

Check current sources

Company processes, eligibility rules, technologies, assessment formats, and dates can change. Confirm time-sensitive information against the employer's current job description, candidate notice, and official website. Do not rely on leaked questions or claims that a particular answer will secure an offer.
